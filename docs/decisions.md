# Decisions

One dated line per decision. Newest at the bottom. The roadmap lives at the end.

- 2026-11-01: Web game in TypeScript on HTML Canvas 2D with Vite, Vitest, and Playwright. No game engine, no UI framework. Reason: the owner wants to follow every line.
- 2026-11-01: Hosted on GitHub, not GitLab, because Claude Code cloud sessions require it. GitHub Pages serves the build.
- 2026-11-01: Top-down renderer for v1, with the simulation/renderer split so an isometric renderer can replace it later without touching rules.
- 2026-11-01: Inspired by the early-1990s squad-skirmish genre, not a recreation. Simplify freely; borrow no names, art, sound, or text.
- 2026-11-01: v1 features: one skirmish on one hand-made map, five classes with distinct weapons, cover, pause-to-order, downed soldiers and medics, rare instant kills, win/lose screen.
- 2026-11-01: Pause is a single-player affordance, default on, and will be disabled in future versus play.
- 2026-11-01: Repository is public. On GitHub's free plan, Pages hosting, branch protection with required checks, and unlimited Actions minutes are public-only.
- 2026-11-01: Name is Muddle & Mayhem; repo and package `muddle-and-mayhem`. The rival generals are General Muddle (player, blue) and General Mayhem (computer, red).
- 2026-11-01: Simulation arithmetic is limited to `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt`, `Math.floor`, `Math.abs`, `Math.min`, `Math.max`, `Math.imul`, integer bit operators, and comparisons, so a Mac browser and a Windows browser compute identical battles. `npm run lint:sim` enforces it.
- 2026-11-01: The computer opponent is deterministic: a pure function of simulation state and its own seeded stream, issuing commands through the same command log as the player, so replays reproduce its decisions.
- 2026-11-01: Downed soldiers take no damage from splash or flame, and there is no battle time limit; the AI always advances so battles end.
- 2026-11-01: Friendly fire applies to splash and flame only (grenade, dynamite, rocket splash, flame cone). Direct bullets and rocket bodies pass through friendlies.
- 2026-11-01: Each side has 3 medic calls per battle, fixed. A revived soldier returns at 50% HP. The medic cannot be shot in v1.
- 2026-11-01: Launcher, Scorcher, and Blaster hits that would down a soldier roll a 10% instant-kill chance to start; tune by play.
- 2026-11-02: The sim arithmetic guard also flags `document`, `window`, and imports from `render`, `input`, or `ui`, since the spec bans them alongside the math and the check is free.
- 2026-11-02: Vite runs with `appType: 'mpa'` so `vite preview` returns 404 outside the base path, like Pages. Without it the smoke test passed with a wrong base path.
- 2026-11-02: The arithmetic guard is an allowlist: any `Math` member other than `sqrt`, `floor`, `abs`, `min`, `max`, `imul` is flagged, so newly added or overlooked functions (`asinh`, `round`) cannot slip through.
- 2026-11-02: The ban on the original game's names applies to code, assets, UI text, README, and CLAUDE.md. The design spec and plans under `docs/superpowers/` may name it, since they document what we are deliberately not copying.
- 2026-11-02: Tick rate 30 Hz, tile 32 world units. Crossroads is 40 by 22 tiles (1280 by 704 units), mirror-symmetric left to right.
- 2026-11-02: HP 100, move speed 3.2 units per tick (3 tiles per second), soldier collision radius 12.
- 2026-11-02: Commands carry explicit soldier ids. `select` is a real command that records the side's selection in state so replays show it, but orders never read it; the input layer fills `soldierIds` from the selection.
- 2026-11-02: `step` mutates the state in place. Soldiers, projectiles, and medics keep `prevX`/`prevY` from the previous tick so the renderer can interpolate.
- 2026-11-02: Soldiers auto-fire at enemies in range while moving or holding (`FIRE_WHILE_MOVING = true`). Plan 3's playtest may flip the constant.
- 2026-11-02: Attack order: approach until the target is in range (and in line of sight for direct and flame weapons), then stop and fire; when the target is downed or dead the order becomes hold.
- 2026-11-02: Downed and dead soldiers do not block movement and are never hit or targeted. Dead soldiers stay in the array with status `dead` and their position so the renderer can draw ash.
- 2026-11-02: Splash is full damage to every standing soldier within the radius, both sides, no falloff. A direct projectile deals its direct damage to the soldier it hits and splash damage to everyone else in radius.
- 2026-11-02: Direct projectiles stop at the first cover tile or standing enemy on their path and vanish at maximum range without detonating.
- 2026-11-02: Lobbed shots are a timed arrival: on the landing tick, a splash check at the landing point regardless of cover.
- 2026-11-02: Flame is a cone check each tick: in range, inside the cone (dot product against a precomputed cosine), and in line of sight. Continuous damage per tick, no cooldown.
- 2026-11-02: Instant kill: a Launcher, Scorcher, or Blaster hit that would down a soldier rolls `INSTANT_KILL_CHANCE` (0.1) on the battle stream; success sets status `dead`.
- 2026-11-02: Gunner jam: each shot rolls `GUNNER_JAM_CHANCE`; on a jam the cooldown becomes `GUNNER_JAM_TICKS`.
- 2026-11-02: Aim scatter: every direct and lobbed shot offsets its aim point by a random direction from the 16-entry unit-vector table and a random distance up to the weapon's `spread`.
- 2026-11-02: The medic is abstract: it appears at its side's map edge level with the body, travels in a straight line at `MEDIC_SPEED` ignoring cover, and cannot be hit. The call is spent when the command is accepted.
- 2026-11-02: Win is checked at the end of every tick; a side with zero standing soldiers loses. Both at zero on the same tick is a draw (`winner: 'draw'`).
- 2026-11-02: A move to a blocked tile snaps to the nearest open tile (ring search). A soldier that makes no progress for `STUCK_TICKS` counts as arrived when its destination is within `ARRIVE_SLACK`, otherwise re-plans with other standing soldiers' tiles treated as blocked, and holds if that fails.
- 2026-11-02: State hash is 32-bit FNV-1a over the UTF-16 code units of `JSON.stringify(state)`. ECMAScript specifies number-to-string conversion, so the hash is engine-independent; the rng's functions are skipped.
- 2026-11-02: The computer opponent lives in `src/sim/ai.ts` (not `src/ai/`) so the arithmetic guard covers it. It thinks every `AI_THINK_TICKS` (15) and issues a command only when it would change a soldier's order, so logs stay small.
- 2026-11-02: Soldier names are five original names per side in `constants.ts`: blue Pip, Bert, Maud, Fitz, Dot; red Grub, Nell, Otto, Sal, Wick.
- 2026-11-02: `%` is treated as outside the sim allowlist; sim code uses `imod(a, b) = a - floor(a / b) * b` from `vec.ts`.
- 2026-11-02: Line of sight recomputes tile-crossing parameters from the grid edge at each step instead of accumulating them, so exact-corner ties compare equal and sight lines never cut corners; verified symmetric over every tile-centre pair on Crossroads.
- 2026-11-02: Stuck detection measures the previous tick's net displacement after separation and cover push-out, not the raw path step, so a soldier shoved back counts as stuck; the first tick of a new order is skipped.
- 2026-11-02: Cover push-out adds a 1e-6 margin so the overlap predicate is reliably false after a push.
- 2026-11-02: The map edge counts as cover for projectiles: a round heading off the map resolves as a cover hit at the border, with splash for rockets, rather than vanishing.
- 2026-11-02: On an exact tie between a soldier hit and a cover hit, the soldier takes the hit.
- 2026-11-02: A `damage` event records the amount requested, not the hp actually removed.
- 2026-11-02: The AI's cover bonus counts only real cover tiles, not the map edge, so Launchers are not drawn to the border.
- 2026-11-02: Battle lengths with the starting constants are 10 to 22 seconds of game time (about 300 to 650 ticks); tuning is Plan 3 playtest territory.
- 2026-11-02: The rng's 32-bit state now serialises into the hash via a `JSON.stringify` replacer, replacing the earlier "rng is skipped" behaviour; this closes the gap where two states differing only in rng position hashed equal.
- 2026-11-02: Direct rounds fly one tile (`TILE_SIZE`) past their weapon's range before vanishing, so a target that steps back at max range can still be hit.
- 2026-11-02: A medic arriving on the very tick the last standing soldier falls keeps that side in the fight, because the medic phase runs before the win check.
- 2026-11-02: `replayBattle` requires the log in ascending tick order and throws otherwise.
- 2026-11-02: Lobbers search for a covered target within `AI_LOBBER_TARGET_REACH` (2) times their range.
- 2026-11-02: Plan 3 layout is one logical canvas of 1280 by 876 pixels: a 40-pixel top bar, the 1280 by 704 battlefield at one pixel per world unit, and a 132-pixel HUD. The canvas scales uniformly to fit the window, letterboxed and `devicePixelRatio` aware, so screen-to-world is one `Projection` plus one `Viewport`; an isometric renderer later replaces the projection and the draw modules only.
- 2026-11-02: The render loop clamps a frame's delta to `MAX_FRAME_MS` (250) and steps at most `MAX_TICKS_PER_FRAME` (8) ticks per frame, keeping the remainder as the interpolation alpha, so a frozen tab resumes without a burst of catch-up ticks.
- 2026-11-02: `src/game/` owns time and the command log. Player commands queue for the next unstepped tick; each tick feeds the queued player commands first, then the red AI's, and logs them in that order, so `replayBattle(seed, log, tick)` reproduces what was played. A Vitest case asserts this with scripted player commands.
- 2026-11-02: Selection lives in the input layer so it works while paused, and every change is mirrored into the sim as a `select` command. Downed soldiers can be selected (so `M` has a target); dead ones cannot. Shift-click and Shift+slot add to the selection; left click on empty ground clears it. Clicking a HUD card selects that slot.
- 2026-11-02: `M` calls the medic for the first downed soldier in the selection, one call per press. Right-click on a downed friendly calls the medic whatever the selection. Right-click on a downed enemy or any dead soldier counts as a ground click.
- 2026-11-02: Pause starts on and Space toggles it. The Esc menu stops ticks while open without touching the pause flag, so resuming restores the previous state. Restart keeps the seed; Play again takes a new one.
- 2026-11-02: The result overlay appears the frame the sim sets `winner`, over the frozen battlefield, with soldiers standing per side, time, seed, Play again and Title.
- 2026-11-02: Seeds come from `?seed=N` in the URL when present (the e2e test and bug reports use it), otherwise a random 32-bit value from `Math.random()`, which is fine outside the sim. With a URL seed, Play again uses seed plus one so rematches stay reproducible.
- 2026-11-02: `window.__mm` is exposed in every build, not only dev: seed, tick, paused, winner, screen, selection, log, pending, state, `hash()`, `replayMatches()`, `advanceTicks(n)`, `worldToClient(x, y)`. The Playwright test runs against the production build and the owner can paste a seed and log from the live site into a bug report.
- 2026-11-02: Visual effects (impact rings, hit and muzzle flashes, JAM labels, revive pulses) are derived from `state.events`, drained after every stepped tick, and expire by sim tick, so pause freezes them and several ticks per frame lose nothing.
- 2026-11-02: Orders queued while paused are drawn as ghost markers (a faint dashed line to the move point, a faint ring on the attack target, a faint ring on the medic target) from the session's pending queue, so the player sees the order was taken before unpausing.
- 2026-11-02: Look: 12-unit side-coloured discs with a class letter, facing mark, HP bar and cooldown bar; dashed selection ring, dashed path and attack reticle; downed soldiers are flattened ellipses with a `+` prompt on friendlies while calls remain; dead soldiers are ash. Bullets are dots, rockets dots with a trail, lobbed shots a render-only parabola with a ground shadow, the flame cone a translucent 60-degree wedge, the medic a white disc with a red cross and a dashed line to the body. Palette: blue `#3d7bd6`, red `#d64545`, ground `#3b6b35`, monospace system font, all in `src/render/palette.ts`.
- 2026-11-02: Slot keys are read from `KeyboardEvent.code` (`Digit1` to `Digit5`), not `key`, because Shift+2 reports `@` on a US layout and Shift+slot must add to the selection.
- 2026-11-02: Playtest over 12 seeds and three blue players (the AI, a passive squad, a scripted squad holding the left wall gap): battles last 11 to 22 seconds, first contact at 5 to 10 seconds, fights 6 to 15 seconds after contact; the Launcher downs most, the Scorcher fewest; the AI advances as a tight group, which lobbers punish. Raising `MAX_HP` to 150 or 200 only stretched fights to 12 to 18 seconds and did not change medic use, so no constant was changed; tuning waits for the owner's hands-on play.
- 2026-11-02: `playwright.config.ts` honours `PW_CHROMIUM_PATH` for a preinstalled browser when the local Playwright version has no download of its own. CI leaves it unset.
- 2026-11-02: Screenshots of the title, battle, medic run and result live in `docs/screenshots/` for the README and pull requests; they are regenerated by hand when the look changes.
- 2026-11-02: Plan 4 measures pacing instead of guessing: `npm run measure` (`scripts/measure-pacing.mts`) runs the real simulation AI versus AI over 60 seeds and prints battle length, first damage, downs, medic use and the winner split; it is the arbiter for every pacing claim. The gameplay tunables in `constants.ts` are `let` bindings so `overrideTuning` can try candidate values without editing the file; the game never calls it. `runBattle` takes an optional `onStep` observer. Node runs the script with its own type stripping plus `scripts/ts-resolve.mjs`, which adds the `.ts` extension to the source's extensionless imports; no new dependency.
- 2026-11-02: Baseline before Plan 4 (seeds 1 to 60, AI versus AI): median battle 14.6 s (9.9 to 19.5), first damage 5.4 s, 9.2 s from contact to the end, red spent 11 of 180 medic calls, blue 16.
- 2026-11-02: Soldiers carry `downedTick` (the tick they went down or died, -1 while standing) so the AI, a pure function of state, can tell how long a body has lain there. Revive resets it.
- 2026-11-02: AI medic rule: revive when the body is safe (as before), or when fewer than `AI_MEDIC_DESPERATE_STANDING` (3) are standing, or when the body has been down for `AI_MEDIC_PATIENCE_TICKS` (90, three seconds). The old all-or-nothing safety check was never true in the mid-map brawl the AI creates, so red ended most battles with three calls unused; now both sides spend 180 of 180 over 60 seeds. Bodies are still taken in `AI_REVIVE_PRIORITY` order.
- 2026-11-02: `M` skips downed soldiers that already have a medic run under way, so a second press helps the next downed selected soldier or does nothing. The sim already rejected the duplicate call, so the key looked like it worked while doing nothing.
- 2026-11-02: AI spacing: `bestFiringTile` subtracts `AI_SPACING_PENALTY` (3, more than the cover bonus) from a tile with a standing friendly on any of its eight neighbours, so one grenade rarely catches two. The AI's bunching had let splash weapons account for most downs; spacing is worth about five seconds of battle length and makes the squad look less like a parade.
- 2026-11-02: Plan 4 pacing constants, old to new and why. `MOVE_SPEED` 3.2 to 1.8: first contact moves from 5.4 s to 9.6 s, the goal being about ten. `MAX_HP` 100 to 160: more hits to down for every weapon; Plan 3 found HP alone did nothing, but with the cooldowns below it adds about ten seconds. Gunner cooldown 6 to 18, damage 8 to 5, spread 14 to 40: from 40 damage per second to about 8, with most rounds missing; still the fastest-firing weapon. Launcher cooldown 75 to 270, damage 45 to 35, splash radius 40 to 32, splash damage 25 to 20, spread 10 to 16: it was the top killer. Chucker cooldown 60 to 210, splash radius 48 to 40, splash damage 35 to 30, spread 20 to 36. Blaster cooldown 105 to 300, splash radius 40 to 32, splash damage 70 to 55, spread 16 to 30: still the slowest and the biggest single hit. Scorcher damage per tick 1 to 0.2: 30 damage per second per soldier in the cone becomes 6; hp is fractional after a burn, which is fine for hashing because every engine runs the same IEEE subtraction. Ranges, and the class order of ranges and cooldowns, are unchanged.
- 2026-11-02: Plan 4 result (seeds 1 to 60): median battle 62.8 s (49.9 to 101.1), first damage 9.6 s, 53.5 s from contact to the end, every medic call spent by both sides, winners blue 36 and red 24; seeds 61 to 160 give a median of 64.1 s. A passive blue squad that never moves still loses 60 of 60 (median 59.7 s), so the AI is not neutered. Considered and rejected: HP alone (Plan 3 already showed it does nothing); the task's starting candidate (26 s); halving damage per second again (37 s); single-target weapons as a thought experiment (59 s: the Launcher and Scorcher still decide fights, so bunching is only part of the story); `AI_THINK_TICKS` 30 (two seconds longer, duller AI); a cover-pause advance (contact timing was already right once `MOVE_SPEED` dropped).
- 2026-11-02: Scorcher flame damage per tick 0.2 to 0.5 (6 to 15 damage a second) and range 96 to 72 (issue #8). At 0.2 the Scorcher was the weakest weapon: two nose to nose took about 27 seconds to down each other and the HP bars barely moved. Flame alone trades battle length for Scorcher dominance because the charge from Gunner range to flame range takes under three seconds and the AI never focuses fire on the charger; the shorter range (a little over two tiles) makes the charge longer and the cone read more clearly. Now 15 a second downs a fresh soldier in about 11 seconds of continuous burn and a revived one in about 5. The AI-versus-AI median drops from 62.8 s to about 56 s, accepted because a human sees the charge coming. Measured and rejected: 0.8 and 1.0 per tick at range 96 (49 s and 44 s, the Scorcher taking half the downs), and 0.6 at range 72 with `MAX_HP` 180 (60 s).
- 2026-11-02: Movers yield, holders stay (issue #6). In `separate`, a soldier following a path takes the whole overlap push and a soldier with no path takes none; two movers or two holders still split it half each. Before, every pair split the push, so a soldier walking into a teammate holding behind cover shoved it half a step a tick into the open and kept making progress, and the same shove worked on enemies. Now the mover makes no progress, the stuck rule fires after `STUCK_TICKS` and re-plans with the holder's tile blocked. Rejected: friendlies passing through each other (discs overlap, five soldiers stack on one tile, the enemy-ramming exploit stays). No new constants. Every battle's outcome changes; `npm run measure` shows pacing unchanged within noise.
- 2026-11-02: In-game debug overlay (issue #7): D, or `?debug=1`, draws a translucent monospace panel in the top-right of the battlefield with seed, tick and clock, pause flag, medic calls per side, the state hash and the winner, then one row per soldier (id, name, side, class, rounded HP, status, cooldown, a `+` when a medic run is heading for it, and the order as `hold`, `move to (tx, ty)` or `attack <name>`). It lives in the UI layer (`src/ui/debugText.ts` is pure and unit-tested, `src/ui/debugOverlay.ts` draws), reads the state and the session and writes nothing; the key goes through `interpretKey` as `toggleDebug` and works while paused, on the result screen and with the Esc menu open. The hash is recomputed every frame it is shown, which is cheap enough for a debug view. Off by default; `window.__mm.debugOverlay` reports the flag.
- 2026-11-02: Plan 5 makes tuning cheap instead of tuning: `?tune=<JSON>` in the URL carries the same `TuningOverrides` object `npm run measure` accepts, URL-encoded or not, and `src/game/tuning.ts` applies it through `overrideTuning` before the app or any battle exists; the live bindings in `constants.ts` then carry it across Play again and Restart. `overrideTuning` now validates the whole object first (`validateTuning`, no side effects) so a typo changes nothing, and a rejected parameter starts the battle on shipped balance with the error in the debug overlay and on `window.__mm.tuneError` rather than a broken page. Rejected: a form on the title screen (the URL is what gets pasted into an issue).
- 2026-11-02: A yellow TUNED badge in the top bar whenever any override is active, at a fixed rectangle (`TUNED_BADGE` in `uiConstants.ts`) so the e2e test can read its pixels, and the override JSON in the debug overlay. A screenshot or report from a tuned battle should never pass for shipped balance.
- 2026-11-02: `damage` events carry `sourceId`, the soldier whose weapon dealt the damage. Each side has one soldier per class, so the weapon alone would name the shooter if there were no friendly fire; splash and flame make it ambiguous, and the report must not credit a grenade that caught a friend to the enemy. The event is part of the serialised state, so every state hash changed; run and replay changed together and no test pinned a value. The `impact` event is unchanged.
- 2026-11-02: Battle statistics (`src/stats/battleStats.ts`) are collected outside the sim by watching each stepped state, in the session for play and in the measure script headless, from one module so the two agree. Definitions: a shot is a `shot` event, or for the Scorcher a tick with the cone active (it has no shot event and never will, at thirty a second); a hit is a tick on which a soldier's weapon damaged at least one enemy, so a grenade catching two is one hit and the rate stays at or under 100%; damage is the amount the event asked for, overkill included, as the damage event already records; downs inflicted and instant kills count enemies only, credited to the source of the damage that dropped the soldier; seconds downed count ticks with status `downed`, so the dead add nothing; medic calls count against the class they were spent on; first contact is the tick of the first damage event. The measure script's "downs by weapon" now uses the same attribution, so friendly-fire downs no longer appear in it (about 4% of downs AI versus AI).
- 2026-11-02: The result screen grew to a 760 by 620 panel with the per-class table under the winner line and a third button, Copy report, between Play again and Title. Copy report puts a Markdown block on the clipboard: a summary list (seed, winner, time, first contact, overrides), the table, and the command log as JSON inside a collapsed `<details>` with the `replayBattle` call to rebuild the battle. When `navigator.clipboard` is missing or refuses (an insecure origin, a denied permission) the same text opens in a read-only text area over the canvas, the one DOM element outside it, and the button says so. `window.__mm.report()` and `window.__mm.stats` expose the same.
- 2026-11-02: `scripts/holdline.mts`, `--blue=holdline` in the measure script: a scripted blue squad that plays more like a person than the AI does and never touches `src/sim/ai.ts`. Rules: at tick 0 every soldier walks to a fixed post west of the left wall gap (gunner (9, 8), launcher (7, 10), chucker (9, 12), scorcher (10, 10), blaster (11, 12)); at post it holds and attacks the enemy nearest the squad's centre once more than half the standing squad can fire at it; a soldier whose target slips out of reach holds again, and one more than a tile from its post walks back; it calls the medic for the first downed friendly in `AI_REVIVE_PRIORITY` order that no standing enemy has within weapon range, or for anyone once a single soldier stands; it advances, attack orders on the focus or nearest enemy for everyone, only while it has more standing soldiers than the enemy. No rng, thinks every `AI_THINK_TICKS`, a command only when the order changes. Blue wins over seeds 1 to 60: AI 55%, passive 0%, hold-line 0%. The first post set tried (the road mouth itself) also lost 60 of 60 with one red down per battle; the kept set takes three; with the red Scorcher's flame cut to 0.2 per tick it still loses every battle, so the loss is structural: a squad that holds cannot beat five converging soldiers with three splash weapons under the shipped balance. Kept as a baseline whose downs per battle and length respond to tuning even where its win rate sits at the floor.
- 2026-11-02: The measure script prints, per side and class, shots, hits, hit rate, damage dealt and downs inflicted as totals over all seeds with per-battle medians in brackets, so the same numbers appear headless and in the browser report. `scripts/ts-resolve.mjs` also resolves a folder import (`'../sim'`) to its `index.ts`, which `src/stats/` needed.
- 2026-11-02: Plan 6 design, with the owner: holding a covered position should cost the attacker; it must not be an automatic loss. The hold-line squad's win rate and red downs per battle (`npm run measure -- --blue=holdline`) are the measure.
- 2026-11-02: Cover is shot geometry, not a readable adjacency rule. "A soldier whose tile touches cover takes less splash" was rejected in favour of what the shot physically did: a trace from the burst point to the soldier decides whether cover lies between.
- 2026-11-02: Cover soaks a lobbed shot that lands on top of it: a grenade whose landing point lies inside a cover tile is absorbed, and everyone in its radius takes the reduced fraction.
- 2026-11-02: Plan 6 expectation: the splash rule will move red downs per battle and battle length more than the hold-line win rate; the costly charge is the lever expected to lift the win rate. A random chance for a direct round to strike cover beside its target is not wanted: direct fire already stops at cover.
- 2026-11-02: Hold-line posts re-sited (Plan 6 task 1, no sim change): gunner (11, 8), launcher (8, 10), chucker (11, 12), scorcher (11, 10), blaster (8, 13). The Gunner and Chucker stand on the west faces of the wall arms with the wall between them and the road; the Scorcher waits at the road mouth where a charger through the gap must pass it; the Launcher is rearmost on the road axis with a view down the road; the Blaster has the southern crate at its back and the gap's west end at the edge of its range. Measured over seeds 1 to 60 as blue wins, median length, red downs per battle and blue Blaster hit rate: the Plan 5 posts 0, 57.3 s, 3, 36%; the road mouth (gunner (12, 9), scorcher (12, 11), blaster (11, 8)) 1, 59.3 s, 4, 62%; crate posts (chucker (9, 14), blaster (8, 13), scorcher (11, 10)) 4, 66.3 s, 4, 73%; the arm faces with the Blaster at (10, 12) 5, 60.3 s, 4, 71%; the arm faces with the Blaster at (8, 13) and the Launcher at (7, 10) 9, 67.4 s, 5, 79%; the kept set 16, 71.4 s, 5, 82%. Seeds 61 to 120 confirm the last two at 9 and 10 wins. The win rate is fragile (the Launcher one tile to (8, 9), (8, 11) or (9, 10) gives 0, 0 and 2 wins) while red downs and the Blaster rate hold, so those two are what the posts are chosen by; the win rate is reported. Later Plan 6 targets are read as a lift above this baseline, not above zero.
- 2026-11-02: Cover soaks splash (Plan 6 task 2). `applySplash` traces from the burst point to each standing soldier in the radius with `firstCoverHit`; when a cover tile lies between, or the burst is flagged absorbed, the soldier takes `COVER_SPLASH_FRACTION` (0.5, a tunable) of the splash. A lobbed shot whose landing tile is cover, or off the map (`isBlocked`), is absorbed; a rocket that strikes a crate face bursts at the face and uses the trace alone, and because `firstCoverHit` skips the start tile the near side takes full splash whichever side of the face the impact point rounds to. The `absorbed` flag is a parameter, not an `impact` event field; the `damage` event records the soaked amount, a 0 at fraction 0. Iteration order, rng draws and event order are unchanged. Measured over seeds 1 to 60 at 0.5, 0.25 and 0 (blue wins, median length, red downs per battle): AI versus AI 32, 58.4 s, 8; 31, 59.7 s, 8; 32, 59.7 s, 8 (before: 33, 59.0 s, 8; first damage 9.6 s and the downs-by-weapon split within two of before at every value); passive 0, 54.3 s, 2 at every value; hold-line 13, 69.0 s, 5; 14, 73.1 s, 5; 11, 73.5 s, 5 (before: 16, 71.4 s, 5; seeds 61 to 120 at 0.5 give 10 wins, the same as before). The knob moves nothing beyond noise because it rarely fires: 2.9% of splash damage events are soaked AI versus AI and 8.5% against the hold-line (20% of Launcher splash, 8% of grenades); the AI does not keep cover between itself and incoming bursts, and a grenade scattered around a soldier on a wall face lands inside the wall about a quarter of the time. ## Roadmap

Rough order, owner may reorder. Each is a separate design conversation when its turn comes.

1. Sound effects (synthesized or openly licensed).
2. Bonus crates and score, with score buying extra medic calls.
3. Terrain effects: water disables the Scorcher; destructible cover.
4. Squad selection screen (choose your five, duplicates allowed).
5. Gamepad support (cursor on stick; Xbox controllers via the Gamepad API).
6. Close-combat mini-game when soldiers touch (punch/kick/block).
7. AI difficulty levels.
8. Real art: original pixel sprites, then an isometric renderer swap.
9. Remote two-player versus over the internet (lockstep on the deterministic sim; pause disabled; relay or WebRTC).
10. Campaign map, battle by battle toward the enemy capital.
11. Commando squad (two directly controlled soldiers).
12. Title screen art: portraits of General Muddle and General Mayhem.
13. Per-class HP and speed, so the Scorcher can be fragile or slow as well as short-ranged (a true glass cannon). Today `MAX_HP` and `MOVE_SPEED` are one value for every class.
14. Drag to select: drag a box on the battlefield to select every friendly soldier inside it, alongside the existing click and shift-click. Raised by the owner after a playtest (2026-11-02): managing five soldiers one click at a time is hard at full speed.
